## The idea

Ideas show up while walking, driving or doing the dishes, which are the moments you can't type. EchoMind records a voice note, transcribes it, and tidies it into something you can search later.

## What it does

- Quick capture: one tap, start talking
- Transcription and short summaries
- Search across everything you've said
