## The problem

Small teams run on-call out of a spreadsheet, a pinned Slack message and someone's memory. Then the memory goes on holiday.

## What it does

Rotaweek keeps the rotation where the team already talks: Slack. It knows who is on call this week, nudges the next person before the swap, and turns the handoff into a short, structured note instead of "anything I should know?" at 6 pm on a Friday.

It works alongside the paging tool you already have. It doesn't try to replace it.
